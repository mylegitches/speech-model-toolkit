4 Pauses

Extra silence after punctuation, on top of the voice's own pauses, for everything spoken here (replies, Repeat mode, scripts). Anywhere in the text, <break time="1s"/> or <break time="250ms"/> adds a pause of exactly that length; it's never read out. These pauses only apply where this app speaks, not in Home Assistant or Hermes.

Commas, semicolons and colons are experimental: to pause there, the sentence is split and each part is spoken on its own, so the voice drops at the split. 0 = off.

⬇ .wav

A conversation: give each line a character (voice) and its words. Pause after sets the gap before the next line; ⚙ sets that line's own pauses after punctuation. ▶ on a line plays just that line.
