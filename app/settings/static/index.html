Speaker identification (AI)

For voices imported from files (Voice → Dataset → Import file): the active AI connection reads a few transcribed lines of each person, plus the show's cast, and names who is speaking, so a whole series ends up as one card per character.

Sends file names and a few transcribed lines per person to your AI provider (and the show name/IMDb ID to TVmaze). Needs an active connection in AI connections above.

Speech recognition

Turns your question into text after the wake word, on this machine (faster-whisper). Only used with an AI connection.

Otherwise it downloads the first time you ask a question.

Audio

Microphone, speaker and volume are remembered by this browser and used by every tab.

Echo cancellation, noise suppression and gain apply to wake word listening. Voice recordings for training always use the raw microphone.
