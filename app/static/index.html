Microphone access

Browsers only allow the microphone on localhost or HTTPS. From another machine, tunnel with ssh -L 8765:localhost:8765 <server>, or add this address to chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure.

Where things are stored

Everything lives in ./data next to docker-compose.yml: wakeword/ (training assets), wakeword-models/ (your wake words), and voice/ (recordings, training runs, exported voices).
